About the department

The Department of Computer Science & Engineering (Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning) at the Institute of Engineering & Management, Kolkata, offers a four-year B.Tech programme focused on machine learning, deep learning, data science and their applications.

Department events are planned by a student committee under faculty guidance. Every event listed on this site is open to students across all branches unless stated otherwise.
